 A mechanism can be real while a particular bot is unprofitable. None of these ten

 The conclusion
 The strongest direction is a small portfolio of specialized trading processes, each exploiting an
 identifiable source of mispricing or compensation. The research does not establish a deployable
 cash cow. It does identify ten falsifiable candidates, three near-term priorities, and one unusually
 interesting new opportunity in perpetual futures.

 What changed after this review
 Sethi's work supports evaluating forecasts by the trading decisions they induce, with explicit risk
 preferences. It does not imply that a more accurate model necessarily earns more money. The best
 fit is a specialist that combines an independent forecast with market information and abstains
 unless the disagreement survives costs. Less prominent markets deserve more attention than the
 headline contracts everyone studies.

 The immediate allocation
 Concentrate research on (1) neglected-market hybrid forecasts, (2) station-specific weather
 nowcasts, and (3) selective liquidity-reward allocation. Give perpetuals a short, separate feasibility
 screen for account economics and executable latency. Do not spend heavily on speed infrastructure
 before that screen passes. Preserve the NFL engine as a benchmark while resolving the Q7
 implementation conflict.

 01 Neglected-market hybrid
 forecasts
 FIRST PRIORITY | HISTORICAL EMPIRICAL SUPPORT; CURRENT EDGE UNTESTED

 Best strategic fit: a narrow specialist with permission to abstain, not a
 general-purpose model that trades every disagreement.

 The edge and its payer
 Specialize in markets where an independent, disciplined forecast captures information that the
 marginal trader has not fully processed. Combine it with market prices instead of mechanically
 opposing them. The proposed source of profit is uneven research attention, not the belief that
 markets are generally foolish.

 What supports it
 The 2025 Sethi-coauthor study reports models losing in prominent presidential markets while some
 outperform in less visible markets; the uploaded deck displays that contrast. The 2021 precursor
 finds benefits from combining models and markets. SAGE supports skill- and recency-sensitive
 human-machine aggregation, but tests forecasting scores rather than trading profit. These are
 reasons to investigate selective hybrids, not a guarantee that low attention implies inefficiency.
 [S01-S03, D1]

 Adaptation for this project
 Choose one coherent family with defensible external information: initially selected down-ballot
 elections, or another family only after verifying comparable forecast inputs. Create a timestamped
 probability distribution with market-only, model-only and hybrid baselines. Weight forecasts using
 past out-of-sample calibration; shrink weak evidence toward the market. An LLM can extract and
 organize public information, but its unsupported confidence is not a probability model.

 Economics and capacity
 Trade only when conservative expected payout exceeds the executable cost plus all fees, a
 model-error allowance and a capital charge. A 54% estimate against a 50c ask gives only 2.25c
 before other costs under an illustrative standard 1.75c taker fee. Election capital can remain locked
 for weeks; apparently large percentage gaps may generate little annual dollar capacity.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Freeze one family and its forecast sources before observing future outcomes. Record revisions and
 orderbooks at actual receipt time. Compare the three baselines under identical feasible fills, then
 test the hybrid on untouched events. Reject if its gains disappear against market-only, are
 concentrated in one correlated event, or exist only at midpoints. Treat this as a slower research
 stream; one election cycle is not hundreds of independent experiments.

 02 Station-specific weather
 nowcasts
 HIGH PRIORITY | SETTLEMENT MECHANICS VERIFIED; FORECAST ADVANTAGE
 PROPOSED

 The defensible asset would be a clean station-to-settlement dataset and calibrated
 residual model, not access to a common weather API.

 The edge and its payer
 Estimate the distribution of the final official daily high or low at the exact settlement station,
 conditional on the weather already observed and what can still happen. The potential counterparty
 error is using a generic city forecast, a rounded app observation or an unconditional morning
 distribution after the day has substantially unfolded.

 What supports it
 Kalshi's weather guide distinguishes final NWS daily climate reports from hourly products and
 explains the local-standard-time day. NWS explains how five-minute averages and Celsius
 conversion can make displayed temperatures differ from official climatic extremes. These establish
 a real modeling problem; they do not establish that current prices systematically mishandle it. The
 repo's weather scaffolds do not yet provide a profitable out-of-sample execution study. [S08, S09,
 R2]

 Adaptation for this project
 Start with a few stations rather than every weather market. Model the remaining-day maximum
 conditional on the observed maximum, time, clouds, wind shifts, humidity and forecast changes.
 Map raw observations into the official measurement and rounding process. Learn station- and
 regime-specific residuals. Treat missing or stale feeds as missing evidence; the NWS Phoenix
 experimental page itself currently warns about outages and erroneous preliminary values.

 Economics and capacity
 Prefer situations with a meaningful probability gap, not a one-tick race. There is daily repetition, but
 neighboring strikes and cities can share weather risk. Prices in apparently impossible buckets are
 not free money when the observation feed differs from the settlement source. Account for
 settlement lag and the chance of losing all principal on an incorrect near-certainty trade.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Archive original forecasts, receipt-time observations, exact station rules, books and eventual final
 reports for an initial 60-day pilot, then reserve future days for evaluation. Compare against the
 market and a simple forecast-error distribution. Sixty days is a collection target, not proof of power.
 Reject if extra skill fails to improve executable net returns, rounding explains the apparent edge, or
 profits depend on one season or station.

 03 Selective liquidity-reward
 allocation
 HIGH PRIORITY | PROGRAM VERIFIED; REPO GAP OBSERVATIONS; NET EDGE
 UNTESTED

 Potential small, repeatable supplement; currently weak evidence for a broad cash
 cow. Do not annualize an ideal hourly rate from one snapshot.

 The edge and its payer
 Allocate resting liquidity only where the expected exchange reward exceeds adverse selection,
 inventory loss, fees and capital use. Kalshi funds the subsidy. This is a scheduling and competition
 problem across eligible markets, not a forecast-free passive-income strategy.

 What supports it
 The current program scores qualifying depth on both sides and pays proportional shares over
 eligible snapshots. The repo's AMS004 survey found 32 of 199 usable markets short of target depth.
 Of 29 later-screened one-empty-side cases, 16 would make a 1c order cross; only 13 could rest.
 Three passed an idealized remaining-reward-versus-$10-loss screen. This validates sparse
 candidate supply, not a durable income stream. [S10, R6]

 Adaptation for this project
 Continue the existing census/tape work instead of launching a duplicate snapshot study.
 Recompute the reference level and reward share after adding our proposed order, and track
 competitors, program windows, eligibility and minimum payment. Rank candidates by net reward
 per constrained capital-hour. Inventory acquired by a fill changes the decision: the bot must not
 replenish losing exposure indefinitely to preserve reward share.

 Economics and capacity
 If one trader owns all qualifying score on one side, its ideal share is half the pool over the relevant
 time, not the whole pool. A 1c bid for 1,000 contracts risks $10 principal on a full losing fill, plus
 costs. The three saved candidates' ideal gross rates were only about $0.32-$0.65 per hour.
 Repeated losing fills, competition or short windows can dominate that.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Measure duration of reward gaps with a forward tape, not just frequency in a snapshot. Simulate
 orders entering the score denominator and carry inventory honestly. Keep a no-reward P&L control
 and a realized-reward ledger. Reject markets whose conservative reward is below expected
 inventory loss, whose qualification depends on unverifiable assumptions, or whose gaps disappear
 before an order can rest. Small pilot orders would require a separate trading decision after research
 approval.

 04 Stale quotes in subsidized
 perpetuals
 HIGHEST CONDITIONAL CEILING | NEW MECHANISM EVIDENCE; ACCESS AND
 SPEED UNPROVEN

 Worth an immediate feasibility screen. Its potential is large only conditional on a
 demonstrable speed advantage and viable account economics.

 The edge and its payer
 A faster trader identifies when a committed market maker's resting perpetual-futures quote is stale
 relative to executable fair value. A liquidity subsidy can keep the maker quoting despite selection
 losses. Our proposed trade monetizes that selection loss while controlling hedge, basis and exit
 costs.

 What supports it
 Sethi's September 23 commentary discusses volume generation through this mechanism. Kalshi's
 September 22 explanation says the questioned trades involved profitable takers against a maker
 and describes liquidity obligations plus a temporary fee holiday for qualifying perpetuals
 self-clearing members. This is useful first-party mechanism evidence, not an independent audit of
 participant returns. The article explicitly says a circulating +0.3/-0.3 basis-point fee program is not
 live. [S11, S12]

 Adaptation for this project
 Separate this from binary BTC betting. First verify the actual account's fee tier, any self-clearing
 requirements, capital contribution and accessible venues. Record synchronized external executable
 bids/asks, Kalshi perps books and source/receipt timestamps. Test whether a signal remains
 actionable after our realistic order-arrival delay, not merely whether one feed leads another.

 Economics and capacity
 Perps fees apply to notional exposure; opening, closing, hedging, funding and basis matter. A fee
 holiday available to an institution is not automatically available to this account. Margin leverage
 does not increase the underlying edge and can magnify losses. The repository's external spot-data
 access failure blocks a local feasibility claim; it does not disprove the mechanism. [S13, R6]

 First validation and rejection rule
 Run a brief read-only feasibility study before infrastructure spending: obtain valid feeds, establish
 costs, and count stale quotes still present at conservative arrival times. Report attainable depth and
 hedge slippage. Reject if the apparent spread disappears at arrival, fees consume it, or profitable
 execution requires inaccessible membership economics. Any later execution study must distinguish
 failed orders from assumed fills.

 05 Calibrated anti-longshot
 market making
 EMPIRICAL PRIOR | HISTORICAL KALSHI PATTERN; CURRENT REPLICATION
 REQUIRED

 A defensible inventory style if replicated. The historical result does not justify a
 universal "buy 90c favorites" bot.

 The edge and its payer
 Supply liquidity to demand for low-probability, exciting outcomes only when their price exceeds a
 conservative calibrated probability. Buying NO is one way to express the view; blindly buying every
 favorite is not the strategy. Potential compensation comes from lottery-like demand and liquidity
 provision.

 What supports it
 Burgi, Deng and Whelan's January 2026 paper studies Kalshi data through April 2025 and
 documents favorite-longshot bias and better maker than taker outcomes. Its historical fee regime
 and its restriction to markets open at least 24 hours limit transfer to today's fast markets. The
 evidence is an empirical prior, not an executable maker backtest for our queue or a guarantee that
 the bias remains. [S14]

 Adaptation for this project
 Estimate calibration by family, horizon, price, liquidity and maker/taker side. Use the existing FL
 harness only after loading accepted real data and actual fee versions. Require an independent
 probability check and adverse-selection model. Avoid treating 20 correlated longshots on one
 underlying event as 20 diversified positions.

 Economics and capacity
 A hypothetical NO at 95c with a true 98% win probability has 3c gross edge. At the illustrative
 standard taker multiplier, its unrounded fee is 0.3325c, leaving 2.6675c before other costs. If its
 true probability is only 95%, the same trade loses after fees. A high win rate can hide occasional
 losses large enough to erase many wins.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Preregister price bands and market families, then evaluate future settlements and fill-conditional
 returns. Compare equal-risk maker, taker and abstention policies with the same underlying signals.
 Attribute return separately to calibration, spread and rewards. Reject if the estimated bias is
 eliminated by current fees, selected fills lose more than unselected quotes, or tail concentration
 dominates the return.

 06 Official-source interpretation
 PROMISING INFORMATION HYPOTHESIS | AVAILABILITY MUST BE VERIFIED PER
 CONTRACT

 The initial product is an eligibility-and-interpretation test. If markets close before
 the information arrives, stop that family immediately.

 The edge and its payer
 Translate an official release into the exact contract outcome more reliably and quickly than traders
 reacting to a headline. Candidate difficulties include fiscal versus calendar periods, reported versus
 adjusted earnings, growth-rate definitions, rounding and the contract's source hierarchy. The
 proposed payer is a trader who interprets the wrong number or remains uncertain after the correct
 one is available.

 What supports it
 The project’s all-market triage retained company-source interpretation while finding that its tested
 CPI contract closes before the release. That blocks the simple post-release CPI-sniping story. SAGE
 supports disciplined evidence aggregation, but supplies no proof of a latency-trading edge. This
 card is a proposed adaptation with a clear gate: the relevant information must arrive while the
 specific market can still execute. [R6, S03]

 Adaptation for this project
 Build a small registry of contract definitions and approved public source URLs. Before each event,
 identify the exact field or sentence needed. Archive the first received version of the release and
 deterministic extraction output. Use an LLM for ambiguous-text triage, with explicit abstention; use
 numerical parsers and rule checks wherever possible. Never treat a revised web page as if it was
 available earlier.

 Economics and capacity
 The opportunity may last seconds or minutes, but event frequency and available depth may be
 small. The best niche could be semantic difficulty rather than raw network speed. A price
 approaching certainty still carries source, interpretation and settlement risk. Fees and failed orders
 belong in the measured result.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Screen the first 20 candidate releases for actual open-market overlap before building a strategy.
 For those that pass, record source arrival, interpretation completion, book updates and attainable
 prices; compare a simple headline parser with the rule-aware parser. Reject any family with no
 tradable information window, no incremental semantic advantage, or unresolved rule ambiguity.
 Move to untouched future releases before claiming returns.

 07 Correlation-aware combo
 quoting
 NATIVE MECHANISM VERIFIED | JOINT-PROBABILITY AND EXECUTION EDGE
 PROPOSED

 A potentially scalable specialist business, but a later build than weather or reward
 measurement because model and inventory demands are materially higher.

 The edge and its payer
 Quote packages of outcomes when a calibrated joint model can price the requested combination
 more accurately than competitors while charging enough for inventory and selection risk. A
 plausible payer is a customer seeking a convenient or appealing package. The edge would be joint
 pricing and risk management, not multiplication of marginal probabilities.

 What supports it
 Kalshi supports dedicated combo markets and RFQs. Its documentation describes private maker
 responses and shorter confirmation/execution windows for combos. The repo already recognizes
 combo-versus-leg-product measurement, but does not establish a profitable dealer. Official rules
 validate an execution route; they do not show that existing dealers misprice correlation. [S15, S16,
 R2]

 Adaptation for this project
 Begin with one sport and a limited set of combinations whose dependence can be estimated.
 Simulate common underlying game states so that leg probabilities and joint probabilities are
 internally consistent. Track pushes, nonparticipation and fractional leg payoffs exactly. Quote only
 packages for which the full requested size fits portfolio limits; RFQ acceptance is not a guaranteed
 neutral hedge.

 Economics and capacity
 If both legs have probability 0.60, independence gives 0.36, but a plausible positively dependent
 joint probability might be 0.45. Selling the package at 0.40 then has negative expected value
 despite looking expensive relative to 0.36. Marginal legs generally do not replicate a conjunction
 exactly. Fees, confirmation timing, leg liquidity and common-factor exposure can overwhelm
 apparent pricing differences.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Collect accessible RFQ opportunities and contemporaneous legs under actual account permissions.
 Fit the joint model before evaluation, then compare it with independence and a conservative
 dependence bound. Test accepted-quote selection rather than all displayed opportunities. Reject if
 correlation uncertainty spans the quote margin, executable volume is inaccessible, or residual
 unhedgeable inventory supplies all apparent profit.

 08 Anchored flow-dislocation
 trading
 HISTORICAL MICROSTRUCTURE SUPPORT | SIGNAL IDENTIFICATION UNPROVEN

 Useful only with a credible anchor. "A whale moved the market" is an observation,
 not a trading thesis.

 The edge and its payer
 Trade temporary price pressure from urgent or strongly directional demand only when an
 independent value estimate and related markets remain stable. The proposed profit source is
 compensation for absorbing inventory until pressure recedes. This is a conditional mean-reversion
 hypothesis, not a claim that large traders are uninformed.

 What supports it
 Rothschild and Sethi's Intrade study describes heterogeneous strategies, including market making,
 arbitrage and directional activity. Sethi's commentary emphasizes that a large order can reveal
 information or reflect belief, and the deck discusses reflexive effects. Those sources motivate
 distinguishing trader pressure from news; they do not provide a ready-made whale-fading signal.
 [S04, S05, D1]

 Adaptation for this project
 Use public book and trade information to detect abrupt imbalance, consumed depth and divergence
 from a preregistered anchor. Do not invent private trader identities from anonymous Kalshi prints.
 Require source monitoring and coherent comparison contracts. Let the independent model veto
 reversion trades when news explains the move; add explicit time and inventory limits.

 Economics and capacity
 Spreads and short-horizon reversals can look attractive before fills. A maker is most likely to fill
 when its quote has become bad, so quote-level reversion is not the relevant return. Measure
 conditional adverse movement after attainable fills. Position capacity is bounded by both event risk
 and realistic exit depth; a marked gain in an illiquid book may be uncollectible.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Preregister a small number of imbalance/anchor conditions and compare with matched no-shock
 periods. Use future events, conservative queue assumptions and news receipt times. Test whether
 reversion exceeds costs after actionable arrival, not at the detected extreme. Reject if effects
 vanish when news is controlled, only one event drives the result, or forced exits produce negative
 net returns.

 09 Crypto settlement-window
 pricing
 BENCHMARK DATA MECHANICS VERIFIED | REMAINING-UNCERTAINTY EDGE
 PROPOSED

 A sharply defined falsifiable variant worth testing; no basis yet to resurrect a
 generic short-term crypto bot.

 The edge and its payer
 Price a short-horizon binary using the exact settlement average and the part of that average
 already observed. The target is a trader or model treating the contract as a bet on a final spot tick
 or using the wrong reference feed. This differs from a generic BTC momentum or indicator bot.

 What supports it
 Kalshi now documents an authenticated CF Benchmarks feed with raw index values, trailing
 averages and final-minute quarter-hour accumulation. The once-per-second documentation
 specifies boundary conventions; the five-Hz channel has raw ticks rather than those averages. That
 supports a rule-faithful data pipeline. It does not establish that the market ignores the same public
 information. Contract-specific rules still govern settlement. [S17, S18]

 Adaptation for this project
 For a contract whose rule is an equal-weight average of 60 specified observations, track the fixed
 sum S after k observations. To finish above strike K, the remaining observations must sum to more
 than 60K-S, subject to the contract's tie and rounding rules. Model that remaining joint path
 distribution; do not count correlated seconds as independent draws or replace it with the final tick.

 Economics and capacity
 Most of the uncertainty can disappear near close, but so can accessible price discrepancies. Many
 participants have the same feed, fees consume small margins, and missing ticks can create false
 near-certainties. Trading and settlement timestamps must be distinguished. A public benchmark
 stream alone is not a speed advantage.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Reconstruct archived outcomes from the precise source and compare against settled contracts
 before estimating profit. Then compare spot-only, full-window and partial-window models on future
 windows at realistic order arrival. Require real books and fee versions in the existing BTC harness.
 Reject if replay cannot reproduce settlement, incremental calibration does not survive costs, or fills
 are assumed after trading has closed.

 10 Rule-proven event baskets
 MATHEMATICAL SUPPORT | NEGATIVE STATIC SCAN; CONDITIONAL BACKLOG

 Keep as a selective structural check and backlog item. The existing negative scan
 makes it a poor first use of additional build time.

 The edge and its payer
 Exploit a price inconsistency among contracts with a proven relationship: a threshold implies
 another threshold, deadlines nest, or a complete set partitions all outcomes. Potential profit comes
 from fragmented attention or the service of completing an inconvenient basket.

 What supports it
 Payoff inequalities are exact when the rules truly imply them. But the project's AMS static scan
 found no positive gross floor in 180 depth-complete cases from 216 candidate cases. That is
 evidence against promoting the already-tested static taker scanner. Sethi's work on arbitrage limits
 also cautions that apparent price differences can persist because implementation is costly. [R6,
 S06]

 Adaptation for this project
 Keep only genuinely new relationships or a distinct execution policy, such as patient basket
 completion during transient imbalance. Prove the relationship with identical source, timestamp,
 threshold convention and resolution hierarchy. Do not conflate YES/NO reciprocity with two
 independent legs. A paper deadline implication can fail if contracts use different definitions of
 occurrence or announcement.

 Economics and capacity
 For A contained in B, YES(B) plus NO(A) pays at least $1, provided their binary rules are compatible.
 Buying those legs for 49c and 48c appears to lock 3c before fees, but illustrative standard taker
 fees total about 3.4965c, making the floor negative. Maker quotes may improve arithmetic but
 introduce non-atomic fills, unmatched inventory and queue risk.

 First validation and rejection rule
 Before collecting more data, specify what differs from the failed static scan. Test new rule classes
 separately from execution changes. Track matched and unmatched legs, cash usage and
 liquidatable value with shared depth. Reject if the floor is not positive after all costs at feasible size,
 if rule equivalence is uncertain, or if expected unmatched losses consume the completion margin.

 The accounting that decides
 survival
 ONE SHARED CAPITAL POOL; EXECUTION-CONDITIONAL RETURNS

 Optimize risk-adjusted net dollars per constrained capital-hour, with uncertainty
 and capacity reported beside the headline P&L.

 Binary expected value
 For a fully paid YES purchase held to a binary settlement, expected net value per contract is q - p -
 net fees - other costs. Here q must be the probability conditional on the information and the fact
 that our order fills. A maker's unconditional forecast can be right while its filled orders are
 systematically bad. Exit-based strategies must model realized entry and exit cash flows instead of
 replacing them with terminal payoff.

 Fees must be versioned
 The July 7 event-contract schedule gives model formulas with market multipliers; maker fees are
 not universally zero. Illustrative unrounded standard-multiplier taker cost at 50c is 1.75c. Current
 API documentation distinguishes direct-member balance precision of $0.0001 from non-direct
 precision of $0.01 and describes per-order rounding accumulators. The repo's older cent-ceiling
 convention requires a version-and-account reconciliation, not an assumption that one formula
 covers every historical and current trade. Prefer actual recorded fee components. [S19, S20]

 Separate the profit sources
 Report trading P&L excluding rewards, exchange rewards actually earned, fees, inventory
 liquidation or settlement, and capital cost. Do not hide a negative forecasting strategy inside a
 positive subsidy. Likewise, do not count an ideal reward estimate as cash received. For perpetuals
 use notional-based fees, funding and hedge costs, not the binary fee formula.

 Capacity is a first-class output
 For each candidate record executable dollars per day, independent opportunities, capital-hours,
 stress drawdown and correlation with existing positions. A 20% return on $5 of available depth is a
 curiosity; a modest repeatable edge with substantial capacity could be more valuable. A quote can
 disappear, and consuming it changes the opportunity.

 No duplicate liquidity or fake diversification
 A public print cannot be credited as a fill to multiple bots, and competing bots must share cash,
 queue and exit depth. Contracts on one election, game, weather system or crypto price path share
 risk. Event-cluster uncertainty matters more than the raw number of fills or timestamps. Never
 annualize a reused-week replay into an account-growth claim.
