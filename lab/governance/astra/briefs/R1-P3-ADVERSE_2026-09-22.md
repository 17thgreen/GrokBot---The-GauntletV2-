## Named risks (Shin / de-vig / overlap)

### 1. De-vig method bias (Shin vs proportional) — leakage / prompt drift
- Proportional de-vig and Shin de-vig are **not interchangeable** \(p_{\mathrm{fair}}\).
- R1 source notes Shin was a **silent bias** in the polymm autopsy.
- **Gate:** freeze must name which de-vig (or both, as a factorial arm). Switching method after seeing fills/outcomes = drift. Mixing methods without an arm label = leakage into “the” fair.

### 2. Fee blindness at the hedge
- Paired lock “YES+NO cost < 1” must be **fee-aware** once R1-P1 fee algebra lands; raw touch sum ignoring maker/taker round-up is not lock EV.
- Until P1 Examiner tests exist, any hedge_complete claim stays **modeled under stated fee assumption**, not venue truth.

### 3. Capacity / completion fantasy
- `hedge_complete_flag=false` residuals are directional inventory, not “almost arb.”
- Do not roll incomplete hedges into completed net; track open residual separately (Working Plan §3).

### 4. Lookahead / odds freshness
- `odds_age_sec` must use the odds timestamp available **at decision time**, not a later scrape backfilled onto the quote.
- Markout that peeks future odds beyond the declared +Δt window = lookahead.

### 5. Dead-overlap with Q6/Q7 (strategy drift)
- Combined pair acquisition economics already live in Q6/Q7. Using this packet to change pair admission, margin filter, or allocator without a **new freeze ID** = silent retune — **banned**.
- Allowed uses only: (a) Adversary/Examiner **scorecard demand** on any sports maker claiming external-odds edge; (b) **post-Q7** external-odds measurement arm under its own freeze.

### 6. Venue mismatch / evidence laundering
- Polymarket CLOB ≠ Kalshi microstructure. Algebra port only.
- Author blog / wallet P&L is anecdote — **not** Astra evidence, not a kill criterion for incumbent, not a promote criterion for challenger.

### 7. Regime break
- Odds API outages, sportsbook suspension, or delayed lines inflate `odds_age_sec` and toxic fill rates — stress cases required before any shadow claim that external-odds edge survives live public tape.
