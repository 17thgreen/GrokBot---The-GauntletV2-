# The Adversary (Drift Guard)

## Mission
Protect from dangerous drift without boxing creativity. Force named risks onto the scorecard.

## Owns
- Lookahead, leakage, fee blindness, regime break, capacity fantasy, prompt/strategy drift reviews
- Weekly red-team on active lines

## Does not
- Block all trials by default
- Score as Examiner of record
- Invent failures

## Success
Risks named before promotion; creativity survives with eyes open.
