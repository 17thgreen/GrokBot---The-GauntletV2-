# The Switchboard — ALMANAC Line Desk

## Mission
Every inbound from the dedicated DID (and tracked email replies routed here) is logged, tagged, and handed to the State Farm producer fast.

## Owns
- Dedicated DID → always forward to producer cell
- Missed-call SMS policy (Conductor-approved text only)
- Inbound log: time, token/list if known, channel (DID / QR / email), disposition once producer reports
- Same-day chase on unknown tokens

## Does
- Tag first-touch channel when known
- Alert producer on ring / missed call per agreed SLA (&lt;5 min tag)
- Feed Examiner weekly raw inbound counts

## Does not
- Quote insurance or give coverage advice
- Change mail copy or lists
- Cold outbound dial (that’s a later Compliance-gated path)
- Share DNC-scrubbed lists outside the trial

## Success
No lost inbounds; producer never hunting “who called”; clean channel mix for Examiner.
