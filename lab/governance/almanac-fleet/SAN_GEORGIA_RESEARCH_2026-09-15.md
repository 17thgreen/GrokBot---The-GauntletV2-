### Logan’s fee intuition vs official math

- Georgia has **10 active** NPAs (see §2).  
- Free: 5. Remaining statewide: **5 paid**.  
- **If subscribed before FY2027 fees take effect (2026-10-01):** 5 × $82 = **$410/yr**.  
- **If subscribed on/after 2026-10-01:** 5 × $85 = **$425/yr**.  
- Logan’s band **~$380–425/yr** is consistent with **5 paid GA codes** at current/near-term published rates (not a separate Georgia fee schedule).

**Timing note:** Research date is **2026-09-15**. FY2026 rates still apply until **2026-10-01**. Subscribing the five paid GA codes before that date locks the annual period at **$410** for those codes (subscription year runs from first day of the activation month).

## 3. Recommended first-5 free set (Atlanta-heavy + path to statewide)

### Recommended free five

**404, 470, 678, 770, 943** — the full Atlanta metro overlay complex.

### Why this pick

1. **Compliance geometry, not marketing preference:** Under 16 CFR § 310.8, you may not call into an NPA unless that NPA is on your subscription. Atlanta is one numbering-plan area served by **five** coextensive overlays. New wireless / VoIP numbers increasingly land on **470** and **943**. Skipping any Atlanta overlay leaves a hole: a manual call to a 943 (or 470) number without that NPA subscribed is a TSR risk even if 404/770 were scrubbed.
2. **ALMANAC sequence is Atlanta-heavy:** Homeowner/landlord mail → email → manual call will concentrate in metro Atlanta. Putting all five free slots on Atlanta maximizes lawful call coverage where volume is highest **at $0**.
3. **Statewide is the paid layer:** Remaining GA NPAs **706, 762, 478, 912, 229** (5 codes) → **$410** (FY2026) or **$425** (FY2027). Portal also supports “all area codes within a state,” which for Georgia would include these after the free five are accounted for in fee math.
4. **Do not “spread” free slots geographically** (e.g., 404 + 770 + 706 + 912 + 478) if you will actually dial Atlanta overlays you did not buy — that undercuts compliance. Breadth without the overlays you call is worse than metro-complete + paid remainder.

### Paid remainder (for full Georgia)

| Codes | FY2026 (before 2026-10-01) | FY2027 (on/after 2026-10-01) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 706, 762, 478, 912, 229 | **$410** | **$425** |

Optional later: subscribe **565** when live (would add another paid NPA unless still within free five — it will not be, if Atlanta five are free).

### Steps to register and get a SAN (Seller)

Summarized from FTC Q&A Q15, Q21–27, Q39–43:

1. Open **https://telemarketing.donotcall.gov/** → **Register New Users** / create profile.  
2. Designate an **Authorized Representative**.  
3. Choose organization function: **Seller** (typical for the entity whose goods/services are offered — e.g., the agency/seller in the ALMANAC funnel). Use **Telemarketer/Service Provider** only if that is the correct role; TM/SP still needs each Seller’s own SAN to call for that Seller.  
4. Enter identifying information: organization name, address, contact phone/email; **EIN** (nn-nnnnnnn) **or**, if sole proprietor with no EIN, **SSN**.  
5. **Certify** (under penalty of law / perjury language on the form) that access is solely to comply with TSR / prevent calls to Registry numbers.  
6. Receive **Organization ID** on-screen; confirmation email with Representative and Downloader passwords → click activation link → log in.  
7. **Create New Subscription** → subscribe to area codes:  
   - Prefer **Area Codes by Area Code Number** for the free five: **404, 470, 678, 770, 943**; **or**  
   - **All area codes within a state** → Georgia (then pay for codes beyond five).  
8. If ≤5 codes and not Exempt: up to **one business day** processing (FTC Q27; Q49 notes up to **3 business days** in one place — plan for a short delay). SAN appears under **Manage/Renew Subscriptions** → Check Status / View Area Codes. **SAN is not emailed.**  
9. If >5 codes: continue to **Pay.gov**; credit card → SAN typically same session; ACH → wait until payment clears (often 3–5 business days).  
10. Download Full List / Change List or use Interactive Phone Number Search (≤10 numbers per lookup; 100/session). Re-scrub **≤31 days** before calls.

### Identity, EIN, payment, attestations required

| Item | Required? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization legal name & address | Yes | Profile identity |
| Authorized Representative name, phone, email | Yes | Law-enforcement / help-desk contact |
| **EIN** | Yes unless sole prop | Format nn-nnnnnnn |
| **SSN** | Only if sole owner/prop with no EIN | Highly sensitive |
| Organization function (Seller / TM-SP / Exempt) | Yes | Wrong “Exempt” claim risks civil/criminal exposure |
| Certification under penalty of law | Yes | Permitted use of Registry only |
| Payment (Pay.gov) | If >5 NPAs and not Exempt | Card or ACH |
| Passwords | Yes | Representative + Downloader; complex rules |

### Human-only gates (Logan or State Farm agency — not automation)

Do **not** automate or agent-complete these:

1. **Entering EIN or SSN** (tax identity / PII).  
2. **Penalty-of-perjury / TSR certification** — must be a real authorized human for the Seller.  
3. **Pay.gov checkout** — real payment instrument and authorization.  
4. **Account activation** via the org’s real Representative email inbox.  
5. **Deciding Seller vs TM/SP vs Exempt** and whether ALMANAC / agency is the Seller who must own the SAN (legal role judgment; counsel if unclear).  
6. **Sharing SAN** with any dialer/vendor — Seller must consciously share; Seller remains responsible for 31-day scrub.  
7. **Any false or placeholder identity** to “test” the form.

Insurance-agency nuance: If calls sell/offer State Farm products, the **Seller** (typically the agency or entity providing the good/service in the telemarketing transaction) needs the SAN. Confirm with compliance counsel which legal entity is Seller vs any vendor that only dials or scrubs.

## 6. ALMANAC operational checklist (post-SAN)

1. Subscribe free Atlanta five; decide whether to add paid five for statewide **before 2026-10-01** to hold **$410**.  
2. Store Organization ID, SAN, expiration, and last Full/Change download date in governance (not in chat logs).  
3. Scrub cadence: ≤31 days; recommend Full List at least every six months (FTC Q50).  
4. Maintain entity-specific DNC list for opt-outs (safe harbor).  
5. If a vendor dials: share SAN deliberately; verify vendor download dates.  
6. Watch for **565** activation on 912 geography.
