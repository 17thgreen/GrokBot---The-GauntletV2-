## RULE_AFTER (operable replacement)

**Classification:** CALIBRATION / ECE LAW. File **with** AMD-20260913-001. `RETROACTIVE: NO`.

### Named estimator (do not invent another)

- **Function:** `expected_calibration_error` in `harness/examiner/src/pm002_market_baseline.py`
- **Bins (`RELIABILITY_BINS`):** \([0.02,0.50)\), \([0.50,0.55)\), \([0.55,0.60)\), \([0.60,0.70)\), \([0.70,0.80)\), \([0.80,0.90)\), \([0.90,0.98)\)
- **Formula:** weighted mean \(|\mathrm{obs\_rate} - \mathrm{mean}_m|\) over bins with \(n \ge\) `MIN_N_FOR_BIN_RATE_CI` \(= 20\)
- **UNTESTED if:** \(N <\) `MIN_N_FOR_ECE` \(= 100\) **or** `powered_bin_count` \(<\) `MIN_POWERED_BINS_FOR_CAL` \(= 2\)
- **Default comparison:** model ECE vs market ECE on the **same cell**, same estimator, same bins

### Power / display rule (no power theater)

- The power rule is a **minimum DISPLAY rule**, not a published-power claim.
- Thin → **`UNTESTED`**. Never a pass. Never a silent skip on promote.
- \(N \ge 100\) does **not** prove statistical power.
- Do **not** import a calibrator.
- Do **not** claim N≥100 proves power. Do **not** block Wave 002 pending a calibration-theory paper.
