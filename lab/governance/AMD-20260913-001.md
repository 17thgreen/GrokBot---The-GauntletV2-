## RULE_AFTER (operable replacement)

**Classification:** SCORED-FORECAST LAW (applies going forward only; `RETROACTIVE: NO`).

1. **Legal scored \(p_t\):** A scored forecast submitted to the Binary Examiner must satisfy \(p_t \in (\varepsilon, 1-\varepsilon)\) unless Clock has recorded an **algebraic lock** (Map 1). Raw 0/1 maps remain legal as unit-test predicates and diagnostics; they are **not** Examiner inputs.
2. **ε-clip is hygiene, not uncertainty:** \(\varepsilon\)-clipping is numerical hygiene. It is **not** a model of remainder-window uncertainty. Turning \(1\) into \(0.9999\) still asserts near-certainty.
3. **Failed map stays failed:** A failed probability mapping stays failed. A materially different uncertainty map is a **new** Feature Card with explicit lineage and independent evaluation.
4. **No rehabilitation:** This AMD does **not** rehabilitate TEST-20260913-001 or FEAT-005. `RETROACTIVE: NO`.
5. **Same-\(t\) incumbent:** A claim that \(p_t\) beats \(m_t\) is `UNTESTED` unless the mid timestamp equals the decision timestamp, or Clock has recorded why that mid is blocked and named the legal substitute. F2 vs T−1m is not a same-\(t\) beat.
6. **Out of scope of this AMD:** Packet §1.4 learned-model card class is **not** written into this AMD. It lives as run config + Feature template (`archive/templates/FEATURE_LEARNED_PT.md`). Do not treat this AMD as a general fitting license.
