## THESIS
When the touch spread widens beyond a short-horizon baseline because one side of the touch empties (not because both sides pull), and the empty side re-quotes within a brief repair window without a large same-side aggressor follow-through, mid/microprice over the next 5–10 minutes tends to mean-revert toward the pre-blowout mid more than continue in the depletion direction. [H]

## MECHANISM
WHO — a large aggressive taker or fleeting quote cancel that clears the touch, plus market makers re-joining. Mechanical imbalance — temporary wide spread / missing touch on one side. WHY persist — repair of the touch and inventory re-skew often pull quotes back toward the prior mid over minutes. WHY disappear — sustained one-sided aggression, both-side withdrawal (risk-off), or venue latency making repair stale. Distinct from Low-RV continuation: signal is a spread/queue shock and repair, not low-volatility drift. [H]
