WHY PERSIST: short-horizon inventory/queue effects after one-sided small-taker bursts can last minutes. WHY DISAPPEAR: when count imbalance is a rewrite of bar return/volume (cemetery-redundant), or when few large prints dominate. Distinct from CEM-001/002/003 OHLCV claims. [H]
